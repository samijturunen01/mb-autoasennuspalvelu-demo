# Image plan – MB-Autoasennuspalvelu

No authentic photographs of the workshop, people or work could be found online. The demo therefore uses **no photos at all**: the visual language is built from the authentic logo drawing and original line illustrations, and nothing is presented as a photo. There are no temporary stock images to replace.

The items below are **recommended additions** that would strengthen trust before production. None is technically required, and the layout works without them.

| Priority | Page / section | Current visual | Recommended authentic photo | Size / orientation | Subject and composition | Needed before production? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Etusivu → "Kaksi numeroa" people cards; Yritys → "Jouni ja JP" | Outlined monograms "J" / "JP" | Portraits of Jouni and JP in the workshop | 1200×1500, portrait | Waist-up, natural light, workshop background, real work clothes | Recommended |
| 2 | Yritys → intro visual | Logo on graphite plate | Workshop exterior with signage, Kuurnankatu 28 | 2400×1600, landscape | Daylight, entrance and signage visible, so customers recognise the building | Recommended |
| 3 | Vianhaku ja korjaukset → intro visual | Diagnostics line illustration | Mercedes on the lift / diagnostics in use | 2000×1500, landscape | Real customer car only with permission; screens without personal data | Optional |
| 4 | Pakettiautot → intro visual | Van line illustration | A Mercedes van being serviced in the workshop | 2000×1500, landscape | Sprinter or Vito in the bay, no customer branding without permission | Optional |
| 5 | Yhteystiedot → map placeholder | Schematic street lines | Entrance photo to help first-time visitors find the door | 1600×1200 | Where to drive in, where to park | Optional |

Photos are only valuable if they are real. Do not replace the illustrations with stock photos of other workshops.
