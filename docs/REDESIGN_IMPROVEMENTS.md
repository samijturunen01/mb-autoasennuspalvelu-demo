# Digital presence audit and improvements – MB-Autoasennuspalvelu Oy

The company has **no website**. This is a new site, not a redesign. This audit covers its current digital presence (2026-11-19).

| Channel | Finding | Impact |

## Competitive context (brief)

- **Authorised dealer (Wetteri Joensuu, Pamilonkatu 31):** brand authority, online booking and dealer pricing. Independent specialists compete on accessibility and personal service, and on price *(assumption)*.
- **Mercedes-Benz ECO-huolto (Veho):** fixed-price brand service for vans over 6 years old, which shows that van owners compare price and convenience.
- **General independent workshops in Joensuu** (all brands): an MB specialisation is a clear differentiator if it is communicated.
- The KL-ServicePartner chain gives national credibility, but the chain site doesn't tell this workshop's local story.

No claims of superiority over competitors are made on the site.

## Most commercially meaningful improvements delivered

1. A first owned web presence with a clear offer, location and specialisation.
2. Direct, named phone numbers everywhere (header, hero, mobile bar, people cards, footer).
3. A booking request that produces a qualified lead (plate, job, timing, callback number).
4. MB-specific trust content: Huolto A/B explainer, warranty FAQ, independence statement, chain membership.
5. A dedicated van page for business customers.
6. Local SEO foundations: prerendered pages, unique titles and descriptions, AutoRepair, Service, FAQ and Breadcrumb JSON-LD, sitemap.
7. Brand identity preserved: the authentic logo is vectorised and its W124 drawing becomes the site's visual language.
