# Conversion strategy – MB-Autoasennuspalvelu Oy

Verified facts: see `CONTENT_SOURCES.md`. Statements below marked *(assumption)* are strategic reasoning, not verified business data.

## Business model

A small (1–4 people) independent workshop in Joensuu that specialises in Mercedes-Benz passenger cars and vans and belongs to the KL-ServicePartner chain. Revenue comes from servicing and repair labour plus parts. Work is arranged by phone or email, and the car is brought to Kuurnankatu 28. There is no online booking, no public price list and (until now) no website.

## Customer segments

| Segment | Needs and pain points | What they need before contacting |
|---|---|---|
| **Private Mercedes-Benz owners** (cars out of or in warranty) | Want brand know-how without dealer-level distance; worry about warranty, service history and resale value; get a "Huolto A/B" reminder or a warning light | Does this workshop know my car? Is my warranty and service record safe? How do I book? What does it cost? |
| **Businesses with Mercedes vans** (Sprinter, Vito; tradespeople, small fleets) *(assumption: a key revenue segment, given the "pakettiautot" specialisation)* | Downtime costs money; heavy use, winter starts, fuel-operated heaters, tow hitches | Do you service vans? Can I arrange several cars? Who do I call? |
| **Hybrid / EV Mercedes owners** | Unsure whether an independent workshop may touch the car | Are you qualified (e-Pro)? Which jobs can you do? |
| **Seasonal / installation customers** | Block heater, fuel-operated heater, tow hitch | Do you install it on my model? |

Journey stages: *urgent* (warning light → call now), *planned* (service reminder → book), *comparing* (dealer vs. specialist → trust signals, FAQ), *returning* (wants the number fast).

## Verified strengths used on the site

Mercedes-Benz specialisation (cars and vans) · up-to-date diagnostic equipment · two named direct phone lines (Jouni, JP) · electronic service book entries · KL-ServicePartner chain membership + e-Pro network · 21 listed services · central Joensuu address.

## Primary conversion goal

**Service booking request:** a phone call to the workshop or a submitted "huoltoaikapyyntö" form. Phone is the fastest path for a 1–4 person workshop. The form covers after-hours visitors and collects the registration plate, job and callback number, which makes it a qualified lead.

Secondary goals: direct call to JP or Jouni, email, directions, service page views, van page views, map loads.

## Main conversion barriers (no website today)

1. The company cannot be found or evaluated online. The Squarespace domain shows only "Coming Soon", and directory listings have no phone number for the company.
2. Customers can confuse it with the older Immonen Ky listing at the same address.
3. No explanation of services, specialisation or independence from the brand dealer.
4. Warranty and service-history worries keep owners at the dealer *(assumption)*.
5. No after-hours way to request a time.
6. No visible route to the workshop.

## Design responses

| Barrier | Response |
|---|---|
| Not findable / unclear | Clear H1 "Mercedes-Benzin huolto ja korjaus Joensuussa", local SEO, structured data, unique page titles |
| Confusion with Immonen Ky | Official name, business ID and address on Contact/Yritys/footer |
| Specialist value unclear | "Yksi merkki. Siksi tunnemme sen." section, service plate, Huolto A/B explainer |
| Warranty worry | FAQ with an accurate statement of EU block-exemption rules, attributed chain statements, "independent – not authorised dealer" clarity |
| Hard to book | Phone in header, hero and sticky mobile bar; 3-step booking form with plate input and topic chips; topic preselection from every service CTA |
| Location | Click-to-load Google Map, directions and "Avaa Google Mapsissa" buttons |

## Customer journey on the site

Hero (offer + 2 CTAs + trust strip) → specialist reasons → service plate → Huolto A/B → vans → direct numbers + hours → chain trust → FAQ → closing CTA. Every service page ends in a topic-preselected booking CTA.

## Conversion hypotheses (to test after launch – not proven)

- Visible direct numbers for named people increase calls compared with an anonymous switchboard number.
- A topic-preselected form, which arrives with the plate and the job, produces more qualified requests than a free-text contact form.
- The Huolto A/B explainer and the warranty FAQ reduce hesitation among owners who would otherwise go to the dealer.
- A separate van page attracts business customers searching for "Sprinter huolto Joensuu".
